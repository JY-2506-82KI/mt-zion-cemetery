Evelyn Christina “Chris” Iler, departed this life on June 27, 2019 at the age of 78. She was born in Winona, Missouri, the daughter of Loyd and Bernice (Brawley) Lay.
She is preceded in death by her husband, parents, five brothers and one daughter.
She is survived by children, Terry Eveland, Kenneth Iler, Jr., James Timothy “Tim” Iler, and Kristol France; children she raised, Misty Iler and Andrea Iler; two sisters, Bessie Jordan and Mary Pochan; eight grandchildren; thirteen great-grandchildren; and multiple nieces and nephews.
Evelyn was a member of New Testament Church of Jesus Christ in Winona, Missouri. She was best known as “Aunt Chris” to most everyone who knew her. She lived a life devoted to God and to family.
Funeral Services will be held Monday, July 1, 2019 at Yarber Mortuary chapel, Winona, Missouri at 11:00 a.m. The family will receive friends at 10:00 a.m. until service time. Interment will be in Mt. Zion Cemetery, Winona, Missouri.
