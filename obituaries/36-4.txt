J.H. (Horner) Norton of Winona, Missouri passed away in Poplar Bluff Hospital Monday evening, following a stroke.  We understood Mr. Norton suffered a stroke Sunday and was rushed to the hospital, where every effort was made to save his life, but in vain.  His age was about 63, we are informed.  
Funeral arrangements are incomplete as we go to press. 
Horner Norton was a man who made many friends.  He was well and widely known throughout this section, and many mourn at his passing.
