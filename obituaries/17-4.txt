Bessie Jane Pyrtle was born September 15, 1926 in Dixon, MO to John Franklin Melton and Rose (Klepzig) Melton. She passed away July 12, 1997 at her daughter's home in Harrison, AR at the age of 70 years.
She was united in marriage to Merlin George Pyrtle on February 4, 1946 in Hardy, AR. There were two sons and three daughters born unto this marriage.
Bessie is preceded in death by her parents; her husband Merlin on July 2, 1981; three brothers, and two sisters, and one son-in-law, Larry Herd.
She is survived by her children, Dale Pyrtle and wife, Jennie of Doniphan, MO, Wayne Pyrtle and wife, Barbara of Halltown, MO, Velta Sagers, and husband John Harrison, AR, Wanda Herd of Ozark, MO and Lois Brawley and husband Tom of Ozark, MO; one brother, Charles Melton of Washington State; Five sisters, Georgie Jones of Ava, MO, Ethel Sartin of Winona, MO, Anabel Smith of Bush, CO, Maudie Goforth of Fenton, MO, and Judy Scharpf of Mountain View, MO; and several grandchildren and great grandchildren and great grandchildren.
Bessie will be sadly missed by all who knew and loved her.
Funeral services were held at 11:00 am, Tuesday, July 15, 1997 at the Duncan Chapel in Winona with Mike Bloomquist officiating. Interment was in the Mount Zion Cemetery under the direction of the Duncan Funeral Home of Winona.
