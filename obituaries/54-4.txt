Alma Lea (Johnston) Russell was born December 13, 1934 in Winona, MO to Manuel M. Johnston and Lona Bell (Hill) Johnston. Alma Lea passed away on December 5, 2009 at the age of 74 years.
She was united in marriage to James Wesley Russell on August 17, 1949 in Mammoth Springs, AR; and to this union, there were four children born, Charlotte, Michael, Cris and Cindy.
She is preceded in death by her parents, Mack and Lona; husband, James; five brothers, Wilson, Earl, Elmo, Jesse and Ralph; and one sister, Velma Goff.
Alma Lea is survived by a son, James Michael Russell and wife, Velma, of Winona, MO; three daughters, Charlotte Sue McDonald and husband, Richard, of Republic, MO, Margaret Cristelle Stanfill and husband, Terry, of Gentry, AR and Cynthia Lea Martin and husband, Sean, of Gentry AR; one brother, Charles Johnston and wife, Vickie, of Protem, MO; nine grandchildren; seven great-grandchildren, and a host of other family and friends.
Alma Lea was a strong caring person who always put her family first. She is loved by all her family and friends and will be truly missed.
Visitation was held from 6-8 p.m. on Wednesday, December 9, at Duncan Funeral Home, Winona, MO. There will be a graveside service at 1:00 p.m. on Thursday, December 10 at Mt. Zion Cemetery, Winona, MO.
