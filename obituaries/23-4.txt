Richard Zimri Albert was born July 26, 1882 in Reynolds County, Mo., to Eliza and W. M. Albert, and departed this life January 14, 1957 at his home in Winona, at the age of 74 years, 5 months and 20 days.

He was united with the Methodist Church at an early age.

Zim was united in marriage to Bessie Lona Hurst on July 11, 1904. To this union nine children was born, Marvin of Jerseyville, Ill., Ernest of Smelterville, Idaho, Earl of St. Louis, Jewel Gust of St. Louis, Palma Sugg of Madison, Ill., Velma Hicks of Winona, Vrel of Winona and two children preceeded him in death. Also surviveing are 11 grandchildren and one Great grandchild; three sisters, May Brawley of Madison, Ill., Edith Bell of St. Louis, and Pearl Barnes of Centralin, Washington, and a host of other relatives and friends.

South Missourian-Democrat
January 24, 1957
