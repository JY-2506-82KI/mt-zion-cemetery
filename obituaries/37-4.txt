Henry A. Vaugh, son of the late Mary and Jackie Wampler, was born in Illinois, December 8, 1877 and passed away at his home in Winona, Mo., on April 24, 1959, at the age of 81 years, four months and 16 days. 
He leaves to mourn his passing his wife, Mrs. Mary Frances Vaugh; five daughters and one son.  One son preceded him in death.  The children are:  Mrs. Grace Sawyer, Jackson, Mo.; Mrs. Daisy Mayberry, Mrs. Velma Haynes, Mrs. Maude Brawley and Mrs. Mary Sallee, all of Winona; and Gale Vaugh, of Edgerton, Kansas; twenty-one grandchildren, and fourteen great-grandchildren.  He also leaves two brothers and two sisters: Andrew Wampler of Doe Run, the Rev. Fred Wampler of St. Louis, Mrs. Effie Hard of Farmington, and Mrs. Bob Burgess of Esther; other relatives and a host of friends.
Mr. Vaugh was a member of the Assembly of God Church in Winona.
Those who attended the funeral from here were Andrew Wampler and children, Leman Hammond, Mr. and Mrs. Al Triplett, Jim McDanual, Mr. and Mrs. Henry Bowyer and Mr. and Mrs. Allen Vaugh.

The Daily Press Leader
Farmington, Mo
May 8, 1959
