Allen Dwain Brawley, 16 year old son of Mr. and Mrs. O.H. Brawley of Winona, Mo. died about 4:00 a.m. Monday morning at West Plains Memorial Hospital as the result of injuries sustained in an accident on Highway 19, some six miles north of Alton Saturday night, about 11:00 p.m.
Young Brawley was a passenger in a 1954 Chevrolet coach driven by David Weaver, 17, of Winona.  Weaver lost control of the car on the dips and curve near the Black Jack School house.  The car was reported to have skidded about 100 feet on the pavement before hitting a ditch, and then rolled end over end for about sixty feet.
Brawley's condition was listed as critical from the head injuries as he was admitted to the hospital.  
The driver suffered a fractured arm and bruises and lacerations. The car was considered a total loss.

South Missourian-Democrat
