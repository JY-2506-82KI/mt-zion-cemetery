Mrs. Lelia Anna Frazier, 57 of 100 East Sixty-seventh, North, died Sunday at St. Augustine, Fla., following a heart attack.  Mrs. Frazier was on a vacation with her husband, L.D. Frazier of the home.  She had been a resident of Kansas City 18 years and was a member of the Gashland Methodist church.
Besides her husband, she leaves a daughter, Mrs. James E. Grider, 209 West Sixty-seventh, North, and three grandchildren.  Services and burial will be Thursday at Winona, Mo.

The Kansas City Times
April 12, 1960
