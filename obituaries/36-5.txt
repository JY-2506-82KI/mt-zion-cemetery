James F. Simpson, 32, a self-employed timber worker from Winona, died at 12:30 p.m. Tuesday in John Cochran VA Medical Center.  He had been in a coma for 10 weeks from a reaction to prescribed medication, the family said.  
He was born Aug. 5, 1950, in Winona, where he remained a lifetime resident.  He was a Marine veteran. 
Surviving are a son, James Carey Simpson of Georgia; his mother, Muriel Simpson of Winona; two brothers, David Simpson of St. Charles and Mark Simpson of Winona; and one sister, Carol Roberts of Winona.
Services will be at 10 a.m. Friday at McSpadden Funeral Home at Van Buren.  The Rev. Lowell Moore will officiate.  Burial will be in Mount Zion Cemetery.  Visitation has been scheduled for 6 tonight.

April 21, 1983
