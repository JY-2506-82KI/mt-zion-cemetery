Leola Fay Clemons, 89, of Owensville, died Monday, Dec.16, 2013, at her residence.
Leola was born in Low Wassie, Mo. on April 8, 1924, to the late Samuel Thomas and Frances Viola (Allen) Medley.
She was retired from Paramount Cap Factory in Ellington and had worked at Lost Valley Restaurant and Frene Valley Nursing Home.
Leola was also preceded in death by sister, Grace Ming; brother, Jim Medley; and brothers-in-law, Richard Ming and Martin Baeumner.
She is survived by husband, Loyd Clemons; children, Betty Docksteiner, Sue Johnson and husband Richard, Bob Clemons and wife Sharon, Tom Clemons and wife Pauline and Darryl Clemons and wife Stella; sister, Maxine Baeumner; and by 12 grandchildren, 23 great-grandchildren and three great, great-grandchildren.
Burial at Mt. Zion Cemetery Assoc. in Winona.
