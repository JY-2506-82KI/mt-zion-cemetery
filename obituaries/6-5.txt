Barbara “Diane” Cain, 59, of Piedmont, Missouri, passed away Thursday, March 14, 2019 at Poplar Bluff Regional Medical Center in Poplar Bluff, Missouri. She was born February 1, 1960 in St. Louis, Missouri to Kenneth and Evelyn “Chris” (Lay) Iler.
She was preceded in death by her father.  Barbara leaves behind her mother; significant other, Jess Mock; one daughter, Misty Iler Teague; three grandchildren, Isaiah, McKayla, and McKinzie Iler; two brothers, Kenneth Iler Jr. and James Iler; and two sisters, Terry Eveland and Kristol France.
Funeral services will be held Monday, March 18, 2019 at 2:00 p.m. in Yarber Mortuary chapel, Winona, Missouri with Gene France officiating. The family will receive friends beginning at 1:00 p.m. prior to services, also at the funeral home. Interment will be in Mt. Zion Cemetery, Winona, Missouri under the direction of Yarber Mortuary, Winona.
