Elsie May Pyrtle Weaver, daughter of Walter and Arminda Pyrtle, was born Jan. 27, 1892, near Piedmont, Mo in Wayne County and departed this life July 29,1983 at the age of 71 years, 6 months and 2 days.  

Most of her early years were spend near Low Wassie, Mo.

She was united in marriage to Leonard Otto Weaver Dec. 24, 1911 at Winona, Missouri.  To this union eight children were born:  Mavis Spitzfaden, Raymond Weaver, Helen Jackson, Lydia Ross, and Bill Weaver all of St. Louis, Mo, Leonard Weaver, Jr. of Cameron, Missouri, and Jerry and Pyrtle Weaver of Winona, Mo. She was preceeded in death by her parents, one sister and two grandchildren.

She is survived by her husband, Leonard, eight children, 16 grandchildren, and 12 great grandchildren.  She is also survived by three sisters and four brothers:  Elma Hibner of Memphis, Tenn., Edith McGhee of Springfield, Mo., Waymon Pyrtle, Snoqualmie Falls, Wash., Leonard Pyrtle of Bismarck, Mo., Leland Pyrtle of Chillicothe, Mo., and Merlin Pyrtle of Low Wassie, Mo.

Elsie put her faith in Christ and was baptized into the Christian Church in the year 1916 and remained strong in her faith since that time.  She was also a member in good standing of the Shannon Rebecca Lodge No. 272 for the past several years.  

She will be sadly missed by her family and a host of relatives and friends.  Funeral services were held Thursday, August 1, 1963 at the Winona Christian Church with Rev. Earl Sigler and Rev D. E. Handling officiating.  Burial was in Mt. Zion Cemetery under the direction of the Clary Funeral Home of Winona, Mo.

South Missourian-Democrat
August 8, 1963
