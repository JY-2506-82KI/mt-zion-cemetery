Jerry L. Weaver, 69, son of Leonard Otto Weaver and Elsie Mae Pyrtle Weaver was born on Aug. 20, 1933 at Winona.  He died May 31, 2003 at St. Johns Regional Health Center in Springfield.  
He was preceded in death by his parents; four brothers, Raymond D. Weaver, G. P. Weaver, Leonard Otto Jr., and William “Bill” Eugene Weaver; one son, Richard Earl Weaver; and one daughter, Wanda Gail Weaver, who died just hours earlier in her St. Louis home.  
Surviving are his wife, Anna L. Weaver; two sons, Jerry Ray Weaver of Licking and Justin Earl Weaver of Houston; two daughters Jo Ann Minnich of Winona and Johna Hofstetter of Raymondville; three sisters, Mavis Spitzfaden of St. Louis, Helen Jackson of Kirksville and Lydia Beth Ross of St. Louis; and six grandchildren.  
Services were June 3 at the Boone Creek Baptist Church with Rev Cecil King officiating.  Grave side services and burial were in the Mt Zion Cemetery near Winona under the direction of Fox Funeral Home of Licking.
