Birch Tree City Collector Dies

Mr. Garrett Prewett of Birch Tree passed away suddenly with a heart attack Tuesday, April 15.  His funeral was at the Baptist Church in Birch Tree Sunday at 2 p.m. with pastor, Rev. Berry, officiating.  Burial was in Mt. Zion Cemetery near Winona, under direction of the Duncan Funeral Home.

All the children were here but one, Ronnie, who was in Siagon; he got here Sunday night.  The other children are Willard, Florence and Ruby of San Jose, California, Patsy Hartman of Fortuna, California, Gerald of Grants Pass, Oregon, and Mary Lou Pulliam of Birch Tree.

Shannon County Current Wave
23 April 1964
