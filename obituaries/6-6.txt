Kenneth Neil Iler was born Aug. 29, 1937, at Van Buren, Mo, the son of Yeuell Capps and Ruby Ansel (Roy) Iler and passed away on Aug. 24, 1992 at his home in Winona, Mo., at the age of 54 years.  
He was united in marriage to Evelyn Christina Lay on Oct. 19, 1957, and to this union five children were born.  He was preceded in death by his parents.  
He is survived by his wife, Evelyn Christina, of the home; two sons, Kenneth Iler Jr. of Winona, James Timothy Iler of St. Louis; one beloved nephew, Loyd Ipock; three daughters, Terry Eveland of Fremont, Barbara Cain of St. Louis, and Kristol France of Branson; three brothers, Eugene Iler, David Iler and Donald Iler, all of St. Louis; one sister, Frances Cowin of St. Louis, and five grandchildren.
Kenneth lived and worked in St. Louis until he became disabled in 1985.  He moved to the Winona area in 1987.  He was a member of the Masonic Lodge in Winona.  
The Funeral was held Wednesday, Aug. 26, at the Duncan Funeral Chapel with Rev. Otto Foust and Gordon Buckner officiating.  Interment was in Mt. Zion Cemetery east of Winona.
