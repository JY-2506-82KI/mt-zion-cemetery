MEDLEY, FRANCES, (nee Allen), on Tues., Dec. 9, 1980.  Beloved wife of the late Samuel T. Medley, dear mother of Faye Clemons of Ellington, Mo., Grace Ming, Maxine Baeumner and Jimmy L. Medley, dear sister of Pheobe Goodman of Tenn., our dear grandmother, great-grandmother, mother-in-law, sister-in-law and aunt.  Faithful Christian dedicated to her family, her love and counsel will be sadly missed by all who knew her.  

Funeral from KUTIS Funeral Home 10151 Gravois (AFFTON), Thurs., Dec. 11, 11 a.m. Interment Mt. Zion Cemetery, Winona, Mo., noon Fri. in parlor Wed. 2 p.m.

St. Louis Post-Dispatch
December 10, 1980
