Born March 3, 1903, in Oregon County. Departed this life March 18, 1959, at the age of 56 years and 15 days.

His father, William Atkins, and one infant brother preceded him in death. His mother is now living with a daughter at Eminence.

He was united in Holy Matrimony to Ethel Strain July 26, 1924. To this union seven children were born: Mrs. Gloria Wanda Warren, Mr. Chester Neil Atkins, Mr. Billy Gayle Atkins, Mrs. Dorris Jean Pulliam, all of Winona, Mrs. Barbara West, of St. Louis, and Patsy Ann and Eunice Wave Atkins, of the home.

He leaves to mourn: his wife; children; mother; two brothers, Marshall and Claude Atkins, of Winona; six sisters: Mrs. Wave Dailey, Mrs. Mattie Vanwinkle, Mrs. Madge Bunyard, all of Winona, Mrs. Marie Nash, of Eminence, Mrs. June Patterson, of Birch Tree, Mrs. Alma Spaulding, of St. Louis; 11 grandchildren; and a host of relatives and friends.

He will be sadly missed by all.

Current Wave
April 2, 1959
