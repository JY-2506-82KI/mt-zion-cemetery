Virginia Lee Owens Shehorn, 87 of Alton, was born Feb. 11, 1919 in Winona, MO, and died Nov. 5, 2006 at the Shepherd's View Residential Care in Alton, MO.

She was a member of the Alton Church of Christ.

She retired from the Eaton Corporation.

Survivors include two sisters-in-law, Frances Owens and Helen Markham; nieces and nephews, Diana and Tom Clary, Joan Sturdy, Jackie Beutler, George and Neda Owens, John Owens, Bill and Cheryle Owens, Terry and Allen Crosno, Jo Spacek, Bob and Katie Briles, Susan and Herb Jourdan, John Markham; her special friend, Wilma Pratt; several great-nieces and great-nephews and friends.

She was preceded in death by her parents, John Markham and Ethel (Harris) Markham; two husbands, George W. Owens and Doris Shehorn; two nephews, Robert Owens and Dale Beutler; two brothers, Jack and Allen Markahm; three sisters-in-law; and three brothers-in-law.

The funeral services were Nov. 8 at the Clary Chapel with Bro. Rick Ragsdale officiating. Interment in the Mount Zion Cemetery in Winona, MO, with arrangements under the direction of the Clary Funeral Home.
