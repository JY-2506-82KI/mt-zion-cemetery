Vada Yeager Glass was the first wife of George Luther White. To that union three children were born; Eunice Irene White 2/26/17-2/21/84; Archie White 8/19/15-4/11/16; Gladys White 4/16/19-2/13/20. Vada and George divorced and each married one more time. George Luther to Beulah (Hoffman) White and Vada Yeager to William Glass.
