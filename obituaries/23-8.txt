Lindul Gene Ball, Jr., beloved son of Lindul Gene and Maudeline Yearwood Ball, was born at Waynesville, Missouri, on January 28, 1957, and passed away at Salem, Missouri, on February 14, 1957, at the age of 17 days.

In addition to his parents he leaves to mourn his passing a sister, Thelma May; a brother, James; his grandparents, Mr. and Mrs. James D. Ball of Salem, and Mr. and Mrs. Frank Yearwood of Winona.

Funeral services were conducted February 15, in the Winona Assembly of God Church, A. W. Freese officiating. Interment was in Mt. Zion Cemetery under direction of Warfel of Salem.
