Pascal Dean White, son of the late Luther T White and Viola H (Willard) White, was born on September 18, 1953, in Alton, Missouri. He departed this life on September 29, 2021, in Birch Tree, Missouri. He attained the age of 68 years, 11 days.

Pascal is preceded in death by his parents.

Pascal is survived by his sister, Sandra Swofford (Vernon) of Winona, MO; brother, William White (Mary) of Winona, MO; nieces, Debra Nicholas (Don), Kimberly White of Winona, MO; one nephew Jessie White of Warrenton, MO.

Pascal had resided in Low Wassie, Missouri, most of his life. He enjoyed gardening, mowing grass, and visiting family and friends.

Graveside services will be held on Saturday, October 2, 2021, at 1:00 p.m. at Mt. Zion Cemetery, Winona, Missouri, with Bro. Darrell Jones officiating under the direction of Yarber Mortuary, Winona.
