Walter Eugene Bettis was born June 10, 1878 to the late George Arthur and Martha Elizabeth Bettis at Nashville, Tennessee, and departed this life February 19, 1963, at the age of 84 years, 8 months, and 9 days.  

He was united in marriage to Tena Neal. Later he was united in marriage to Edna Fouts, who survives.  One step-son precedes him in death three years ago.  He leaves besides his wife, one stepson Leo Youngblood, one step-daughter, Oneita Brawley, two step-daughter-in-laws, one step-son-in-law, and step-grandchildren, seven step-grandchildren and one brother, Will; all of Winona; one brother Joe of Fulton, Mo.; two sisters, Verbie Conroy and Katherine Dunnigan of Cincinnati, Ohio; and a host of nieces and nephews and many friends to mourn his passing.  

Services were held at 2:00 P.M. Saturday, February 23, 1963, with the Rev. Lee Brawley officiating.  Burial was in Mount Zion Cemetery under the direction of Clary Funeral Home of Winona.
