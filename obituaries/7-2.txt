Lod D. Frazier, 68, of 100 Northeast Sixty-seventh, Gladstone, died yesterday at the home.  He was born in Fort Payne, Ala., and had lived here 25 years.  He was a retired employee of Firestone Tire & Rubber company.  He was a member of the Gashland Methodist church.  He leaves his wife, Mrs. Sally Ann Frazier of the home; a daughter, Mrs. GloDeen Grider, 209 Northwest Sixty-seventh, Gladstone; two brothers, Patrick Frazier, 5336 North Wheeling, and Ralph Frazier, Warrenton, Or.; a sister, Mrs. Maybell Wolfe, Winona, Mo., and three grandchildren.  Services will be at 9 o'clock Monday at the Newcomer chapel, North Kansas City; graveside services will be at 9 o'clock Tuesday at Mount Zion cemetery, Winona.  Friends may call after 4 o'clock today at the Newcomer chapel and after 6 o'clock Monday night at the Clary chapel, Winona. 

The Kansas City Star
29 June 1969
